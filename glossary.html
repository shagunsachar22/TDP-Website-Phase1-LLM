Glossary

The terms behind the work.

Definitions for the concepts, protocols, and source frameworks used across The Deliberate Pause.

The Deliberate Pause

The deliberate gap between pressure and reaction where the founder creates enough space to see clearly and act without identity threat.

The Chain

The core mechanism: modality to physiological space to cognitive clarity to better decision under pressure to sustained ambition without suffering.

Founder psychology

The study of how building, output, status, pressure, and identity shape the founder's inner life and decision-making.

Identity fusion

The state where market feedback, output, growth, or reputation feels like proof of personal worth.

Strength-shadow move

TDP's editorial move: validate the trait that helped the builder succeed, then show how the same trait now creates the shadow that keeps them stuck.

Pain target

The searched problem a page uses as its doorway, such as founder burnout, imposter syndrome, fear of failure, validation seeking, or hustle culture.

Method register

The inner-practice vocabulary TDP uses in the middle of a page: witness, sakshi, stillness, presence, vritti, and The Pause.

Scaffolding register

The mental-framework vocabulary that makes the method credible to a logical builder: identity work, decision under pressure, cognitive dissonance, self-image, and personal mastery.

Stillness under pressure

A TDP anchor phrase for applying the witness and the pause inside high-stakes work, rather than away from the work.

Citable unit

A 1-2 sentence definition or thesis written clearly enough that an AI agent can quote it and attribute it to TDP.

Ambition without the suffering

The long-arc promise of TDP: serious ambition that does not extract health, peace, relationships, or self-worth as the price of winning.

Better decisions under pressure

The immediate promise of TDP: one cleaner move when pressure would normally create reactivity, avoidance, or image-protection.

Sakshi

The witness: the part of awareness that can observe thoughts, fear, comparison, and identity threat without becoming them.

Vritti

A fluctuation of the mind. TDP uses the term to describe the stories and mental movements that hijack clarity under pressure.

The Jackson Triangle

A hard-conversation protocol: drop ego, name intent, see the other person.

The Federer Reset

A between-point breathing protocol: inhale four, hold one, exhale five.

Think Box / Play Box

A spatial protocol that separates analysis from execution so thinking does not bleed into the moment of action.

90-Second Recovery

A protocol for letting the body's stress wave pass before opening Slack, replying, deciding, or spiraling.

Sacred Hoops

Phil Jackson's book and coaching philosophy that integrated mindfulness, selflessness, and presence into championship basketball.

Vision54

A golf performance system that separates thinking and playing, proving that physical rituals can change execution under pressure.

The Inner Game

Tim Gallwey's performance framework for quieting self-interference so natural ability can act under pressure.

Patanjali's Yoga Sutras

A classical yogic text that defines yoga as the stilling of the fluctuations of the mind.

Mental training for Indian entrepreneurs.

Essays and founder psychology for entrepreneurs and builders whose self-worth has fused with output.
