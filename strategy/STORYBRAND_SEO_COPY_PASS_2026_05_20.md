# StoryBrand SEO Copy Pass — 2026-05-20

Purpose: revise Phase 1 website copy around the India-first SEO strategy while preserving the StoryBrand flow already implemented on the homepage.

## Core Copy Rule

Use high-volume Indian search language as the doorway, then use TDP's sharper brand language as the answer.

| Layer | Copy job | Preferred language |
|---|---|---|
| SEO doorway | Match what Indian searchers ask | Indian entrepreneurs, entrepreneur burnout, imposter syndrome, self-doubt, validation, career path, failure |
| ICP precision | Keep the site from sounding generic | founders, builders, solopreneurs, creators |
| Category | Name what TDP owns | founder psychology, mental training, identity work |
| Method | Explain why TDP is different | The Pause, The Chain, physiological space, cognitive clarity |
| Proof | Make the method credible | spiritual practice, elite sport, Avi's CreditVidya/CRED story |

## Why Not Replace Founder Everywhere?

`entrepreneur` is the better Indian SEO doorway. In the source keyword data it has higher India volume and CPC than `founder`.

But `founder` still carries the sharper psychological context: team pressure, investor pressure, public failure, exit pressure, and identity fusion with the company. The copy should therefore lead with `Indian entrepreneurs` where discovery matters, then use `founders` and `builders` where emotional precision matters.

Recommended phrase:

> Indian entrepreneurs, founders, and builders

## StoryBrand Flow

1. **Hero:** state the offer clearly.
   - Mental training for Indian entrepreneurs.
   - Outcome: build without burning out or turning output into identity.

2. **Problem:** name the external and internal problem.
   - External: burnout, self-doubt, validation loops, failure pressure.
   - Internal: output has become proof of worth.

3. **Guide:** Avi has lived the problem.
   - CreditVidya to CRED, then the body broke.

4. **Plan:** The Chain.
   - pressure -> pause -> physiological space -> cognitive clarity -> better decision.

5. **Authority / proof:** elite sport and spiritual practice.
   - Not as a public tool library in Phase 1, but as proof of the method.

6. **CTA:** read, understand, subscribe.
   - No lead magnet / practice-library CTA in Phase 1.

## Organic Competition Caveat

CPC and volume are not difficulty scores. A keyword can look attractive in India and still be hard to win if Forbes, Healthline, Calm, Headspace, Isha, Mindvalley, Verywell, or major publishers dominate the organic SERP.

Decision rule:

1. India volume: is there real demand?
2. India CPC: is there commercial signal?
3. SERP ownership: are the current winners generic publishers, apps, spiritual incumbents, or startup-specific sites?
4. TDP wedge: can we answer with a founder/entrepreneur-specific angle that incumbents do not?
5. Page type: should this be homepage copy, FAQ, glossary, essay summary, or a future cornerstone page?

This means broad terms like `entrepreneur`, `burnout`, and `psychology` should appear in copy, schema, and FAQ, but the pages should compete through narrower angles like `entrepreneur burnout`, `founder burnout`, `self-doubt as a founder`, `validation seeking for entrepreneurs`, and `failure postmortem without identity collapse`.
