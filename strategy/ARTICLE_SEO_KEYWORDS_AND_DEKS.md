## How To Use These

- Put the dek directly below the article title.
- Use the primary keyword naturally in the title, dek, opening section, metadata, image alt text, and one relevant subheading where it fits.
- Do not repeat the phrase mechanically throughout the article.
- Keep each article focused on one primary search intent. Related terms can appear naturally in the body.
